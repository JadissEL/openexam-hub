For Express Entry, Immigration, Refugees and Citizenship Canada (IRCC) does not use your overall IELTS band as a single “CLB score.” It converts **each skill** — Speaking, Listening, Reading and Writing — to a Canadian Language Benchmark using a published table. That table is for **IELTS General Training**. This article is a reading of the official chart, checked on 26 November 2026. It is **not** an interactive calculator, and it is not a promise about CRS points or an invitation. Confirm the figures on [IRCC’s language-test page](https://www.canada.ca/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/services/immigrate-canada/express-entry/documents/language-test.html) before you submit a profile.

## Which IELTS IRCC accepts

On that page, under “Language tests we accept,” IRCC states: “You must take the **IELTS General Training** option. We don’t accept **IELTS One Skill Retake** for Express Entry.” The same instruction appears on the [IRCC help centre](https://ircc.canada.ca/english/helpcentre/answer.asp?qnum=638): “You need to take the IELTS General Training test.”

That rules out **IELTS Academic** for this programme. Academic is the usual test for university, as [IELTS describes](https://ielts.org/take-a-test/test-types), but it is not the option IRCC tells Express Entry applicants to take. For a fuller comparison of which module to book, see [IELTS Academic or General Training: which one you need for Express Entry](/blog/ielts-for-express-entry-academic-or-general).

It also rules out **IELTS Online**. That product is the Academic test taken at home. The official [IELTS Online page](https://ielts.org/take-a-test/test-types/ielts-academic-test/ielts-online) says it is “currently only available for the Academic test,” that “IELTS Online is not currently accepted for immigration purposes,” and that for visa and immigration purposes you should take the test in an authorised test centre. Computer-delivered General Training at a centre is a different product; do not confuse the two. For fees, dates and booking labels, see [IELTS fees, registration and 2027 test dates](/blog/ielts-fees-registration-2027-test-dates). The [IELTS exam guide](/exams/ielts) covers format and official practice.

IRCC also accepts CELPIP-General and PTE Core for English, and TEF Canada and TCF Canada for French, on the same page. This post is only about the IELTS General Training table.

## The official IELTS General Training to CLB table

IRCC measures English in **CLB**. The most complete IELTS grid on the Express Entry language-test page sits under the **Federal Skilled Trades Program**. It lists CLB 4 through “10 and above.” The IELTS figures in the Federal Skilled Worker and Canadian Experience Class tables on the same page match the overlapping rows.

Column order below follows IRCC: Speaking, Listening, Reading, Writing.

| CLB level | Speaking | Listening | Reading | Writing |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| 10 and above | 7.5–9.0 | 8.5–9.0 | 8.0–9.0 | 7.5–9.0 |
| 9 | 7.0 | 8.0 | 7.0 | 7.0 |
| 8 | 6.5 | 7.5 | 6.5 | 6.5 |
| 7 | 6.0 | 6.0 | 6.0 | 6.0 |
| 6 | 5.5 | 5.5 | 5.0 | 5.5 |
| 5 | 5.0 | 5.0 | 4.0 | 5.0 |
| 4 | 4.0 | 4.5 | 3.5 | 4.0 |

![Official IRCC IELTS General Training to CLB conversion table, with the CLB 7 row highlighted at 6.0 in each skill](/blog/ielts-to-clb-express-entry/ielts-gt-to-clb-table.webp)

*Chart by OpenExam Hub, from IRCC’s Express Entry language test results page, IELTS tables, checked 26 November 2026. Not an official IRCC graphic.*

**How to read a score.** Convert each skill on its own. Use the highest CLB row whose listed score that skill meets. A Listening **7.0** is above the 6.0 listed for CLB 7, but it is below the **7.5** listed for CLB 8, so it is not CLB 8. The same logic applies to a Reading **6.0**, which is CLB 7, not 8 (Reading needs 6.5 at CLB 8). Scores in the “10 and above” row are published as ranges.

Two rows trip people up:

- **Listening at CLB 8, 9 and 10 is higher than the other skills.** CLB 8 needs Listening **7.5**, not 6.5. CLB 9 needs Listening **8.0**. CLB 10 and above starts at Listening **8.5**.
- **Reading is the lower figure at CLB 4, 5 and 6.** CLB 6 Reading is **5.0**, while the other three skills are 5.5.

## What CLB 7 requires

**CLB 7** is 6.0 in each of the four abilities. That level is the first-official-language minimum for the **Federal Skilled Worker Program**, and the English minimum for **Canadian Experience Class** jobs in **TEER 0 or 1**. CEC jobs in TEER 2 or 3 have a lower official minimum of CLB 5 in all four abilities (IELTS 5.0 / 5.0 / 4.0 / 5.0). The **Federal Skilled Trades Program** minimum is CLB 5 in speaking and listening, and CLB 4 in reading and writing.

![IRCC IELTS scores for CLB 7 (6.0 in all four skills) beside CLB 8, where Listening rises to 7.5](/blog/ielts-to-clb-express-entry/clb-7-and-listening-step.webp)

*Chart by OpenExam Hub, from IRCC’s Express Entry language test results page, checked 26 November 2026.*

A high score in one skill does not lift a skill that sits below the minimum. If three skills are at 7.0 (CLB 9) and Writing is 5.5 (CLB 6), you do not have CLB 7 in all four abilities.

An **overall IELTS 6.5** is also not the same thing as CLB 7. The overall band is an average of the four skills, rounded, as explained in [IELTS 6.5 in 30 days](/blog/ielts-6-5-in-30-days). Official IELTS rounding examples include an overall 6.5 with a Writing 5.0 — and a Writing 5.0 is only **CLB 5** on IRCC’s table. Always convert the four section scores, not the headline band.

Why CLB 7 shows up so often on IRCC programme and CRS pages is covered in [NCLC 7 / CLB 7: what TEF Canada and TCF Canada scores you need](/blog/nclc-7-clb-7-express-entry-scores). That post is about the French tests and the level-7 thresholds; the IELTS numbers for the same CLB rows are the ones in the table above.

## How long results last

On the same language-test page, your results “must be less than 2 years old” when you **complete your Express Entry profile** and when you **submit your application for permanent residence**. If you apply with expired results, IRCC says it will refuse the application. If results will expire while you are in the pool, IRCC’s published options are to be tested again, apply before they expire, or decline an invitation and return to the pool.
