There is no single official worldwide IELTS fee, and there is no single official worldwide calendar of 2027 dates. What you pay and when you can sit the test are shown when you choose a country, city and test type on an official booking tool.

## How to see your fee and your dates

IELTS is jointly owned by the British Council, IDP IELTS and Cambridge University Press & Assessment. [ielts.org](https://ielts.org/take-a-test/booking-your-test) says there are more than 4,500 locations in over 160 countries, and that in some countries you can also take an online test.

To book:

1. Open the official [Find a Test Centre](https://ielts.org/test-centres) tool on ielts.org, or a partner portal such as the [British Council booking portal](https://takeielts.britishcouncil.org/book-ielts-test/test-dates-fees-locations) or [IDP’s test-centre finder](https://ielts.idp.com/find-test-centres).
2. Choose the test you need (Academic, General Training, or IELTS for UKVI where relevant).
3. Enter your country and city.
4. Read the fee and the dates that centre is actually offering.

![Four-step diagram: open an official IELTS test-centre finder, choose Academic or General Training, enter country and city, then read the centre’s fee and dates](/blog/ielts-fees-registration-2027-test-dates/how-to-find-fee-and-dates.webp)

*Diagram by OpenExam Hub, based on ielts.org and British Council booking guidance, checked 20 November 2026.*

The British Council is explicit: “[Test fees vary by country and test type](https://takeielts.britishcouncil.org/book-ielts-test/test-dates-fees-locations). To see the exact fee for your country and chosen test, visit the booking portal.” Dates are “available year-round, with tests running on multiple dates each month at most locations,” and “available dates will be shown once you’ve selected your country, city, and test type.” To register with the British Council you need a [valid passport or national identity card](https://takeielts.britishcouncil.org/what-is-ielts/frequently-asked-questions).

## There is no global 2027 date list

We did not find an official, single IELTS calendar that lists every 2027 session worldwide. Official bodies publish dates **per centre**, through the tools above. If a 2027 date is open at your centre, it will appear there; if it is not listed yet, it has not been opened for booking at that centre.

Do not rely on unofficial “IELTS 2027 dates” blogs. Use ielts.org or the British Council or IDP portal for your country.

## Fees vary — including inside the same country

ielts.org centre pages warn that “[prices may vary from time to time and may be subject to country or local tax](https://ielts.org/test-centres/idp-ielts-canada-toronto).” Treat any number below as a snapshot from 20 November 2026, not a worldwide price list.

Official examples we could verify today:

- **India (Mumbai, IDP Education India),** listed on [ielts.org](https://ielts.org/test-centres/idp-education-india-mumbai): IELTS Academic or General Training on computer, **INR 19,000**; IELTS for UKVI Academic or General Training on computer, **INR 19,250**.
- **Canada (Toronto, IDP IELTS Canada Toronto),** listed on [ielts.org](https://ielts.org/test-centres/idp-ielts-canada-toronto): Academic or General Training on computer, **CAD 379**; the “Writing on Paper” option, **CAD 389**.

![Official fee snapshots dated 20 November 2026: IDP Education India, Mumbai, INR 19,000 for Academic or General Training on computer; IDP IELTS Canada Toronto, CAD 379 for Academic or General Training on computer](/blog/ielts-fees-registration-2027-test-dates/fee-examples-india-canada.webp)

*Chart by OpenExam Hub. Figures from the official ielts.org centre pages for IDP Education India, Mumbai and IDP IELTS Canada Toronto, checked 20 November 2026. Fees vary by centre and can change; these are local snapshots, not a worldwide price list.*

The same India figures appear on [IDP IELTS India’s official fee page](https://ieltsidpindia.com/information/ielts-test-fee), which also says the fee is INR 19,000 at centres across India for Academic and General Training (computer or Writing on Paper) until a **revision from 1 January 2027**. It adds that, until 31 December 2026, you can book at the current fee “even for any future test date, including for test dates in 2027.” That notice is for **IDP in India**. Do not assume other countries have the same freeze or the same 2027 increase.

We did not use a United Kingdom example here: two official UK centre URLs we tried on ielts.org returned 404 on 20 November 2026, so we are not guessing a UK price. Look up your own centre instead.

Cancellation, transfer and re-mark (Enquiry on Results) fees also [vary by centre](https://ielts.org/take-a-test/booking-your-test/cancellations-refunds-remarks-transfers). The British Council says you [may pay a penalty depending on how much notice you give](https://takeielts.britishcouncil.org/what-is-ielts/frequently-asked-questions).

## Book the right test

[IELTS Academic](https://ielts.org/take-a-test/test-types) is the usual choice for university and higher education, and can also be used for professional registration. [IELTS General Training](https://ielts.org/take-a-test/test-types) is aimed at migration to countries such as Australia, Canada, New Zealand and the UK, and at work or study below degree level. Speaking and listening are the same on both; reading and writing differ. Always check the institution or government that will receive your result.

For **Express Entry**, IRCC is specific: you must take the [IELTS General Training option](https://www.canada.ca/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/services/immigrate-canada/express-entry/documents/language-test.html). IRCC does **not** accept [IELTS One Skill Retake](https://ielts.org/take-a-test/booking-your-test/one-skill-retake) for Express Entry. IELTS.org also states that the remote [IELTS Online](https://ielts.org/take-a-test) option is “not accepted by visa immigration authorities,” and that visa applicants must test in person.

One Skill Retake is a real product: you may retake one skill within 60 days of a full IELTS on computer test, once per original test, where the centre offers it. UKVI accepts it for Academic and General Training on computer in selected locations, according to [the official page](https://ielts.org/take-a-test/booking-your-test/one-skill-retake). That does not override IRCC’s Express Entry rule. Check the organisation that will see your scores before you book a retake.

You can [sit IELTS as many times as you need](https://takeielts.britishcouncil.org/what-is-ielts/frequently-asked-questions), paying the fee each time.

## Format changes in 2026, and how results arrive

On 5 March 2026 the IELTS partners announced that, [from mid-2026, they would no longer offer a fully paper-based test](https://ielts.org/news-and-insights/updates-to-ielts-test-delivery); tests would be delivered on computer, with exact timelines varying by market, and a “Writing on Paper” option in selected markets (the rest of the test still on computer). The British Council’s FAQ now says it [no longer offers IELTS as a fully paper-based test](https://takeielts.britishcouncil.org/what-is-ielts/frequently-asked-questions). Existing paper-based results stay valid until the end of the recommended two-year period.

[Results turnaround](https://ielts.org/take-a-test/your-results/getting-and-sharing-your-results) depends on the format: IELTS on computer, 1–5 days (ielts.org’s take-a-test page also says results for in-person computer tests are usually available in 1–2 days); IELTS Online, 6–8 days; IELTS for UKVI, 1–5 days. You receive a Test Report Form (paper or digital).

On **validity**, [ielts.org recommends](https://ielts.org/take-a-test/your-results/ielts-scoring-in-detail) that results be treated as valid for **two years after the test**, while organisations may choose a longer period. The British Council says the Test Report Form [is valid for two years from the test date](https://takeielts.britishcouncil.org/what-is-ielts/frequently-asked-questions). For Express Entry, IRCC is stricter in one sense: results must be less than two years old both when you [complete your profile and when you apply for permanent residence](https://www.canada.ca/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/services/immigrate-canada/express-entry/documents/language-test.html). Universities set their own rules — check theirs.
