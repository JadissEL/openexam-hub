Official tables change. Confirm your numbers on [IRCC’s language test results page](https://www.canada.ca/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/services/immigrate-canada/express-entry/documents/language-test.html) and on your attestation before you submit a profile.

TCF Canada and TEF Canada are both approved French tests for Express Entry, but they are scored on different scales. Use this guide for TCF Canada only. For TEF Canada, see [TEF Canada scores to CLB and NCLC for Express Entry](/blog/tef-canada-clb-nclc-express-entry). If you are specifically aiming at level 7, [NCLC 7 / CLB 7: the TEF and TCF scores you need](/blog/nclc-7-clb-7-express-entry-scores) puts both exams’ NCLC 7 bands side by side.

## What TCF Canada is

[IRCC](https://www.canada.ca/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/services/immigrate-canada/express-entry/documents/language-test.html) lists **TCF Canada** (Test de connaissance du français) as an approved French test for Express Entry, alongside TEF Canada. The test is run by [France Éducation international](https://www.france-education-international.fr/test/tcf-canada).

It is not the same test as **TCF tout public**. TCF Canada is the version IRCC names for economic immigration and citizenship applications. If you are building an Express Entry profile, book TCF Canada.

IRCC measures English with the **Canadian Language Benchmarks (CLB)** and French with the **Niveaux de compétence linguistique canadien (NCLC)**. Each TCF Canada skill is converted to an NCLC level of its own. There is no single total that you type into the profile.

OpenExam Hub’s [TCF Canada exam guide](/exams/tcf-canada) summarises the official format.

## IRCC’s TCF Canada tables

These bands are from [IRCC’s language test results page](https://www.canada.ca/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/services/immigrate-canada/express-entry/documents/language-test.html), checked 21 November 2026. The most complete grid on that page — including NCLC 4 through 10 — sits under the Federal Skilled Trades Program. The same NCLC 7, 8 and 9-and-above bands appear in the Federal Skilled Worker first-official-language table, and the same NCLC 5 to 10 bands appear for the Canadian Experience Class. Speaking and writing are scored out of 20; listening and reading are scored out of 699.

| NCLC | Speaking (/20) | Listening (/699) | Reading (/699) | Writing (/20) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 10 and above | 16–20 | 549–699 | 549–699 | 16–20 |
| 9 | 14–15 | 523–548 | 524–548 | 14–15 |
| 8 | 12–13 | 503–522 | 499–523 | 12–13 |
| 7 | 10–11 | 458–502 | 453–498 | 10–11 |
| 6 | 7–9 | 398–457 | 406–452 | 7–9 |
| 5 | 6 | 369–397 | 375–405 | 6 |
| 4 | 4–5 | 331–368 | 342–374 | 4–5 |

Two details are easy to miss. Listening NCLC 9 starts at **523**, while reading NCLC 9 starts at **524**. And NCLC 5 speaking and writing are a single score of **6**, not a range.

If this grid and the NCLC level printed on your attestation disagree, follow IRCC and your official results.

## What level do you need?

It depends on the programme, and sometimes on your job’s NOC TEER. IRCC’s French minima are:

| Programme | French minimum |
|---|---|
| Federal Skilled Worker, first official language | NCLC 7 in all four abilities. Second official language: NCLC 5 (or CLB 5 in English). |
| Canadian Experience Class, TEER 0 or 1 | NCLC 7 in all four abilities |
| Canadian Experience Class, TEER 2 or 3 | NCLC 5 in all four abilities |
| Federal Skilled Trades | NCLC 5 in speaking and listening; NCLC 4 in reading and writing |

Meeting a minimum only makes you eligible. [CRS points](https://www.canada.ca/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/services/immigrate-canada/express-entry/check-score/crs-criteria.html) for a first official language are tabulated in CLB; IRCC measures French in NCLC. On that grid, CLB 7 is **16 points** per ability with a spouse or partner, or **17** without — and the maximum is 32 or 34 at CLB 10 or more. A French bonus adds **25 CRS points** for NCLC 7 or higher in all four French skills plus CLB 4 or lower in English (or no English test), or **50 points** if you also have CLB 5 or higher in all four English skills.

## Validity

IRCC requires results to be less than **two years old** when you complete your Express Entry profile **and** when you submit a permanent residence application. If you apply with expired results, IRCC says it will refuse the application. If a result will expire before you can apply, IRCC’s options are to sit the test again, apply before it expires, or decline an invitation and return to the pool.
