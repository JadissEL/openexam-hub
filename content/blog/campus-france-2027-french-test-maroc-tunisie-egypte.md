This guide is written in **English**, with a short French summary below. Campus France Maroc and Campus France Tunisie publish mainly in French; Campus France Egypt publishes a 2027–2028 brochure in English and in French. English lets us keep the three countries in one comparison, including Egypt, without mixing two full versions.

**Résumé.** Pour la rentrée de septembre 2027, les dossiers Études en France se ferment vite : **11 décembre 2026** pour la DAP en Tunisie (Licence 1, L.AS/PASS, ENSA), **18 décembre** pour le hors-DAP tunisien, **15 décembre à 23 h 59, heure marocaine**, pour toutes les formations connectées au Maroc, **15 décembre 2026** pour la Licence 1, le DUT et l’architecture en Égypte, et **15 janvier 2027** pour les L2, L3 et masters en Égypte. Sauf dispense, un test ou diplôme de français est demandé, et les épreuves acceptées ne sont pas les mêmes selon le pays. Vérifiez le site officiel de votre Espace avant de déposer votre dossier.

**As of 27 November 2026.** Every date and test rule below comes from an official Campus France page or brochure we read on that day. Rules and calendars change, so confirm anything that affects your file on the linked page before you book a test or submit.

This post does not repeat the general [DELF vs TCF](/blog/delf-vs-tcf-which-french-test) comparison, and it is not a guide to sitting [TEF Canada in Morocco or Tunisia](/blog/tef-canada-maroc-tunisie). Those pages stay the place for the diploma-versus-test choice and for TEF Canada centres. What follows is only the Campus France rule in each country for the September 2027 intake.

## The deadlines

Campus France Tunisie’s calendar page is explicit: the 2026/2027 procedure is for **entry in September 2027** (academic year 2027/2028). Morocco’s calendar is titled 2026/2027 and, in the November 2026 leaflet, refers to *bacheliers 2027*. Egypt’s brochure is titled **2027–2028** and opens applications on 1 October 2026 for September 2027.

| Tunisia — hors-DAP | BUT, Licence 2, Licence 3, licence professionnelle, Master 1 and 2, and connected engineering and business schools | **18 December 2026.** Applications opened on 1 October 2026. |

| Egypt — first wave | First-year Licence, DUT and architecture, in the 2027–2028 brochure | **15 December 2026.** Applications opened on 1 October 2026. |

The Tunisia dates also appear on the [platform announcement](https://www.tunisie.campusfrance.org/la-nouvelle-plateforme-de-candidature-etudes-en-france) updated on 7 October 2026, and in the [official calendar PDF](https://www.tunisie.campusfrance.org/system/files/medias/documents/2026-10/calendrier%20EEF%202026_2027.pdf).

Morocco’s calendar page, when we read it, also noted technical problems on the Études en France platform and said candidates would be informed when they were fixed. Check that page if you are filing a Morocco file.

A generic Egypt FAQ still says applications run “until mid-December” for Licence 1 and “until January the 1st” for later years. The **2027–2028 brochure** is more precise, so the dates above follow the brochure, not that FAQ.

## Which French test each country names

![A card for each country describing the French tests named on its official Campus France page](/blog/campus-france-2027-french-test-maroc-tunisie-egypte/accepted-tests.webp)

*Sources: official Campus France pages, checked 27 November 2026. Chart by OpenExam Hub. Universities may still set a higher level.*

### Morocco

[Campus France Maroc’s calendar page](https://www.maroc.campusfrance.org/calendrier-de-la-procedure-de-candidature-20262027) says that, unless you are exempt, a French certification is compulsory: **TCF TP, DELF B2 or DALF**. It adds that a **payment receipt or an exam summons** is accepted. The November 2026 leaflet repeats the same list and says B2 is recommended for study in France; the certifications leaflet adds that some programmes require C1 or C2.

We do not quote fees or remaining exam-session dates from that leaflet: they are set by centres and can change. Book through your local Institut français or another approved centre, and make sure the session is early enough for your file.

### Tunisia

The rules depend on the procedure.

For **DAP** (Licence 1, L.AS/PASS and ENSA), the [Licence 1 page](https://www.tunisie.campusfrance.org/licence-1-dap-blanche-0) says you must provide proof of your French level, and lists:

- a **DELF** (valid for life);
- a **DALF** (valid for life);
- **TCF pour la DAP**, described as TCF Tout Public plus the written-expression paper (valid for two years);
- a **TEF** covering reading, listening, written expression, lexicon and structure, with an optional speaking paper, **at least 14/20 in written expression and a score of 400/699** (valid for two years);
- or a **registration or summons** for TCF, DELF or DALF, if the timetable for processing your file still allows it.

The same page says an existing TCF or TEF attestation must still be valid on the planned start date in France, and names **15 September 2027** as that date.

For **hors-DAP** (including [Licence 2, 3 and licence pro](https://www.tunisie.campusfrance.org/licence-2-licence-3-licence-pro) and [Masters](https://www.tunisie.campusfrance.org/masters-masteres)), the accepted proofs are **DELF, DALF or TCF (SO or TP)**, plus a registration or summons if the timetable allows. Those pages do **not** name the TEF. The same 15 September 2027 validity line appears for an existing TCF.

Campus France Tunisie’s [certifications article](https://www.tunisie.campusfrance.org/les-certifications-en-langue-francaise-0) summarises the same split: TCF pour la DAP or DELF/DALF for Licence 1, L.AS/PASS and ENSA; TCF Tout Public (TP/SO) or DELF/DALF for other applications. It also lists exemptions, including holders of **DELF B2 or DALF C1/C2**, a French baccalauréat, a TEF score of 14/20, and several other cases. The article warns that a university may still ask for a certification even if Campus France grants an exemption. Read that page for the full list rather than treating this paragraph as complete.

### Egypt

The [2027–2028 brochure](https://www.egypte.campusfrance.org/en/campus-france-egypt-brochure-2027-2028) says that for programmes taught in French you should present **DELF B2, DALF C1 or DALF C2, or TCF DAP**. For programmes taught in English it names IELTS, TOEFL or TOEIC. It recommends **B2** in French or English, and says some institutions require C1 or C2. Check each university.

A [language-certifications FAQ](https://www.egypte.campusfrance.org/en/faq/what-are-the-language-certifications-required-tcf-delf-dalf-in-order-to-apply) recommends **TCF DAP with a minimum score of 400 points, or DELF B2/DALF C1**, and again tells you to check the university. If the programme is taught entirely in English and the university does not ask for French, that FAQ says a French certificate is not compulsory.

Campus France Egypt’s [“Evaluate your level of French” page](https://www.egypte.campusfrance.org/en/evaluate-your-level-of-french) states that **B2 is the minimum** for bachelor’s or master’s study in France, and that B2 is required for first-year entry under the DAP. It also notes that DELF and DALF are valid for life and that TCF results are valid for two years.

## How to choose, without mixing the tests up

- **Start from your country page**, not from a general blog comparison. Morocco, Tunisia and Egypt do not publish one shared list.
- **Match the version to the procedure.** TCF pour la DAP is not the same as TCF Tout Public without the written paper, and neither is [TCF Canada](/blog/tef-canada-vs-tcf-canada).
- **A summons is not a result.** Morocco and Tunisia say a receipt or summons can go in the file, but only if the official page still allows it and the timetable still works. Egypt’s application-procedure material also mentions confirmation of registration; do not assume every university will wait.
- **Check the university as well as Campus France.** Several official pages say institutions may ask for a higher level, especially C1 or C2.
- For a lifetime diploma versus a two-year test, the general comparison is in [DELF vs TCF](/blog/delf-vs-tcf-which-french-test). For TEF Canada centres and published Institut français fees in Morocco and Tunisia, see [TEF Canada au Maroc et en Tunisie](/blog/tef-canada-maroc-tunisie).

## What we could not verify

We do not give exam fees or a centre-by-centre session calendar. Those are set by each exam centre and change. The France Éducation international website blocked our automated checks, so we did not use it for dates or prices.

We also did not treat the generic Egypt “when can I apply?” FAQ as the 2027 calendar, because it still says “mid-December” and “January the 1st.” The dates above follow the 2027–2028 brochure.

Morocco’s calendar page mentioned platform problems when we read it. We cannot say whether they are resolved; check that page.
