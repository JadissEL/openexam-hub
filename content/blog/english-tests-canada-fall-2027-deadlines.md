**As of 27 November 2026.** The dates and scores below come from the official undergraduate pages of the University of Toronto, the University of Ottawa and Toronto Metropolitan University, and from IRCC’s Express Entry language-test page, all read on that day. Universities change requirements, and programmes can close early, so confirm anything that affects your file on the linked page before you book a test or submit.

This post clusters those confirmed fall 2027 dates and the English-test scores each university publishes. It does not repeat the full [TOEFL vs IELTS](/blog/toefl-vs-ielts) comparison, and it is not a guide to booking IELTS for immigration. For Express Entry, IRCC wants a different module; that is explained in [IELTS Academic or General for Express Entry](/blog/ielts-for-express-entry-academic-or-general).

## The mid-January cluster, and TMU’s 1 February date

These are undergraduate dates for a **September 2027** start, as each university states them. They are not graduate dates, and they are not a national Canadian deadline.

![Deadline cards for U of T (15 January), uOttawa (recommended 15 January) and TMU (1 February guaranteed consideration), for September 2027 undergraduate entry](/blog/english-tests-canada-fall-2027-deadlines/deadlines.webp)

*Chart by OpenExam Hub. Sources: official U of T, uOttawa and TMU admissions pages, checked 27 November 2026. Not an official document.*

| University | What the official page says | Date |
|---|---|---|
| [University of Toronto](https://future.utoronto.ca/deadlines) | Deadline for most full-time undergraduate faculties, including Arts & Science, Applied Science & Engineering, U of T Mississauga and U of T Scarborough | **15 January.** Many of those faculties also list a recommended early application date of 7 November. |
| University of Toronto — exceptions on the same page | Music: deadline 15 January, with a recommended date of 1 December. Medical Radiation Sciences: **1 February**. Nursing and Physician Assistant also list 15 January, and the page notes that those programmes, and Medical Radiation Sciences, need some university preparation. | See the faculty row |
| [University of Ottawa](https://www.uottawa.ca/study/undergraduate-studies/application-deadlines) | For direct-entry programmes starting in September 2027, uOttawa **recommends** that you submit your application before 15 January 2027. Applications opened on 17 September 2026. | **Recommended 15 January 2027**, not a single hard close date |
| [Toronto Metropolitan University](https://www.torontomu.ca/international/admissions/how-to-apply/program-status-international/) | For September 2027, **1 February 2027** is the guaranteed consideration date for international undergraduate applications to all programmes listed there (Grades-Plus and Grades-Only). The [how-to-apply page](https://www.torontomu.ca/international/admissions/how-to-apply/) says the same. | **1 February 2027** |

A few timing details that sit next to those dates:

- **U of T** also publishes deadlines for supporting documents. Several faculties list **15 January** for the application and a later document deadline, often **1 February**. Engineering lists **15 January** for documents as well. U of T’s English-language page says results must arrive no later than your deadline for supporting documentation.
- **uOttawa** says programmes can close at any time once they reach capacity, and that competitive programmes include Acting, Visual Arts, Music, Social Work and most programmes in Engineering and Health Sciences. It asks you to meet all application requirements, including documents and test results, as soon as possible, **by 15 February 2027, or 30 days after you apply**. For international applicants, it adds that most programmes close in May, to leave time for study-permit processing.
- **TMU** says programmes may close applications before the guaranteed consideration date because of space, and that files received after that date are reviewed subject to space. Its English-language page sets **1 April 2027** as the deadline to provide a test score for fall 2027. It also says your application is not complete without that score, so earlier is safer.

If you missed U of T’s 7 November recommended early date, the 15 January deadline on that page is still the one to work to, unless your faculty row says otherwise.

## The English scores each university publishes

You may not need a test at all. Each university describes exemptions, usually based on years of full-time study in English, and each still asks for a senior-level English course. If a test is required, these are the **IELTS Academic** and **TOEFL iBT** minima on the official undergraduate pages we read. Other tests, such as Duolingo, PTE Academic, CAEL or Cambridge, also appear on those pages; this table stays with IELTS and TOEFL because that is the comparison most applicants ask for.

![Table of official IELTS Academic and TOEFL iBT undergraduate minima at U of T, uOttawa and TMU](/blog/english-tests-canada-fall-2027-deadlines/test-scores.webp)

*Chart by OpenExam Hub. Sources: official U of T, uOttawa and TMU English-requirement pages, checked 27 November 2026. Not an official document.*

### University of Toronto

On the [English language requirements](https://future.utoronto.ca/english-language-requirements) page:

- **IELTS Academic**, including IELTS Online: overall band **6.5**, with **no band below 6.0**. IELTS Academic for UKVI sittings are accepted. U of T does **not** accept the IELTS Indicator or IELTS One Skill Retake. Scores must be achieved on a **single date**. Results must be sent electronically by the test centre to the IELTS Results Service account named “University of Toronto - Undergraduate and Graduate Programs”.
- **TOEFL iBT**, including the Home Edition and the Paper Edition: overall **4.5**, with **4.5 in Writing** and **4.0 in Speaking**, on the scale used for tests taken on or after 21 January 2026. For tests completed **before 21 January 2026**, the minimum is a total of **89**, with **22** in each of Speaking and Writing. Scores must be achieved on a single date. U of T does not use TOEFL MyBest (super-scored) results, and it does not accept TOEFL Essentials, TOEFL ITP or TOEFL ITP Plus for China. The institution code is **0982-00**.

That **4.5** is not a score out of 120. It is the 1–6 scale ETS introduced in January 2026. [TOEFL vs IELTS](/blog/toefl-vs-ielts) explains the change; do not convert it yourself.

### University of Ottawa

On the [language requirements](https://www.uottawa.ca/study/undergraduate-studies/language-requirements) page, for direct entry to an English-language programme, with no language conditions:

- **IELTS Academic** only: **6.5** overall and **6.5** in writing. uOttawa accepts IELTS Academic One Skill Retake. The page also says that an overall score of **6.0** allows you to begin your programme directly, with extra ESL courses.
- **TOEFL**: for tests taken **before 21 January 2026**, TOEFL iBT **86** overall and **22** in writing. For tests taken **after 21 January 2026**, **4.5** overall and **4.5** in writing. The same table still lists “TOEFL Home edition” as **86** overall and **22** in writing; we are repeating that row as the page shows it. uOttawa says it accepts TOEFL iBT, the Special at Home Edition and TOEFL Essentials, and that it accepts MyBest scores for TOEFL iBT. The institution code is **0993**.

Lower scores can lead to a conditional offer with the English Intensive Program. The official page has those bands; this post does not copy every pathway row.

uOttawa also lists language tests it does **not** accept for undergraduate admission, because they are designed for immigration or other non-academic uses: IELTS for UKVI, TCF Canada, TEF Canada, CELPIP, and the Bright Language test.

### Toronto Metropolitan University

On TMU’s [English language requirements](https://www.torontomu.ca/admissions/undergraduate/requirements/english-language/) page, requirements are overall scores. The page says there are **no minimum band scores**, and that the figures are a guideline and subject to change.

- **IELTS Academic**: **6.5** for every undergraduate programme listed in the table. TMU accepts IELTS One Skill Retake results and IELTS Online (Academic). Results must come electronically from the test centre.
- **TOEFL iBT**, including the Home Edition, but not MyBest Scores or TOEFL Essentials. For tests taken **before 21 January 2026**: overall **83** for all Engineering programmes and all Science programmes except Computer Science and Architectural Science, and overall **92** for all other TMU programmes, including Computer Science and Architectural Science. For tests taken **on or after 21 January 2026**: a minimum overall score of **4.5 iBT** in **both** columns. The institution code is **0886**.

## IELTS or TOEFL, for these universities

All three universities accept both IELTS Academic and TOEFL iBT, but they do not treat them identically:

- **Module.** These pages ask for **IELTS Academic**, not General Training.
- **Retakes and combinations.** U of T wants IELTS or TOEFL scores from a single date and does not accept IELTS One Skill Retake or TOEFL MyBest. uOttawa accepts IELTS One Skill Retake and TOEFL MyBest. TMU accepts IELTS One Skill Retake and IELTS Online, and it does not use TOEFL MyBest.
- **Section minima.** U of T and uOttawa set writing (and, at U of T, speaking) floors. TMU’s table is overall-only, with no minimum band scores.
- **The new TOEFL scale.** From 21 January 2026, all three pages give a 1–6 figure, usually **4.5**. Older reports are still read on the 0–120 scale, with different totals at each university.

If both tests are accepted for your programme, [TOEFL vs IELTS](/blog/toefl-vs-ielts) is the place for format and scoring. Then book the test whose rules on that university’s page you can actually meet, including how results are sent.

There is no official worldwide IELTS or TOEFL session list for 2027. Dates are published per centre, as explained in [IELTS fees, registration and 2027 test dates](/blog/ielts-fees-registration-2027-test-dates). Leave time for the score to reach the university before the document deadline, not only before the application date.

## A separate rule for Express Entry

University admission and Express Entry do not share one English test.

On IRCC’s [language test results](https://www.canada.ca/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/services/immigrate-canada/express-entry/documents/language-test.html) page, the approved English tests are **CELPIP-General**, **IELTS General Training** (IRCC does not accept IELTS One Skill Retake) and **PTE Core**. TOEFL is not on that list, and neither is IELTS Academic.

If you also plan to enter the Express Entry pool, you will likely need a second sitting, on the immigration module. [IELTS Academic or General for Express Entry](/blog/ielts-for-express-entry-academic-or-general) and [IELTS to CLB for Express Entry](/blog/ielts-to-clb-express-entry) cover that path. Results must be less than two years old when you complete your profile and when you submit a permanent-residence application.

## What we could not verify

We did not check every Canadian university, and we did not check graduate or professional programmes such as law, medicine or education, which often have their own dates. U of T, uOttawa and TMU also accept tests we have not tabulated row by row, including Duolingo, PTE and Cambridge, with their own minima.

We also did not invent a “safe” booking date before 15 January. Score delivery times depend on the test and the centre. Use the university’s document deadline and the test body’s own result dates.
