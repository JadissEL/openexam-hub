**As of 27 November 2026.** The January and March 2027 dates below come from [Université Clermont Auvergne’s DELF and DALF page](https://www.uca.fr/en/certifications), read on that day. UCA is a worked example, not a national booking portal. Other centres choose their own levels, prices and registration windows, even when they share the same group-exam dates.

This post is about **how to read a centre calendar**. It does not repeat the wider [DELF vs TCF](/blog/delf-vs-tcf-which-french-test) comparison, and it is not a Campus France or Express Entry guide. If you need the diploma for a French-university file, start from the official procedure in your country, then come back here to plan the sitting.

## Two calendars, not one

A DELF or DALF session has two layers:

1. **National group-exam dates**, set by **France Éducation international (FEI)**. UCA states this plainly: “Dates and time of the group exams are determined by France Éducation International (FEI).” The same page notes that, under FEI rules, exams for levels **A2 and B2** take place over a two-day period.
2. **What the centre actually offers**: which levels, which registration or pre-registration window, how to apply, and the fee.

If you only look at the national dates, you can miss the fact that your nearest centre is not offering B2 in January, or that its registration window has already closed. If you only look at one centre, you can miss another centre that is still open for the level you need.

We could not open FEI’s own calendar PDF (the page returned an HTTP 403 from our checker). UCA links that calendar at `france-education-international.fr/document/calendrierdelfdalffrance2026`. Use the centre page you trust, and open the FEI calendar yourself if you need the national grid.

## A worked example: Université Clermont Auvergne

UCA’s Centre FLEURA offers DELF levels **A1, A2, B1 and B2** and DALF **C1**. It does not offer DALF C2 on this page. Each candidate may register for any of the levels the centre is running in that session. UCA also reminds readers that DELF and DALF are national diplomas, recognised worldwide and valid for life.

Two details matter if you are planning January or March.

**January is not a B2 session at this centre.** UCA is only offering A1 and A2 on 12–13 January 2027. If you need B2 or C1 in early 2027, the March session is the one this centre lists. Another official centre may offer different levels on the January dates; that is exactly why you read the local calendar.

**The January pre-registration window is short, and it was still open on 27 November 2026.** UCA’s window runs from 21 to 29 November 2026. If you want that sitting at this centre, the page says you must email `delfdalf.pi@uca.fr` during that period, with your last name, first name, requested level, email address and phone number. Requests sent outside the pre-registration period are not processed. After the window closes, UCA emails you to say whether the application has been accepted. Only then do you receive the registration form, the payment procedure, and a request for a scan of a valid ID. An exam notice is sent at least 15 days before the exam.

For March, the same procedure applies, with pre-registration from **30 January to 7 February 2027**. That window is the planning date, not 16 March itself.

## Fees at this centre only

UCA publishes these registration fees, payable only once registration has been confirmed at the end of the pre-registration period:

- DELF A1: €130
- DELF A2: €130
- DELF B1: €160
- DELF B2: €200
- DALF C1: €220

Those figures are **UCA’s**. They are not a national price list, and they should not be reused for an Institut français or another university centre.

## How to read any other official centre page

When you open a centre calendar, it helps to look for the same five lines UCA makes easy to find:

1. **Exam dates** for the session, which should match the national group dates if the centre follows FEI.
2. **Levels offered in that session**, not the full A1–C2 range the centre might offer across the year.
3. **The registration or pre-registration window**, including time zone if one is stated, and whether late requests are refused.
4. **How to apply** (an email address, an online form, or an in-person desk), and any document you must attach.
5. **The fee for your level at that centre**, paid only when the centre says so.

If a page is undated, or if it still shows last year’s sessions, do not guess. Write to the centre, or use another official centre whose 2027 calendar is published.
