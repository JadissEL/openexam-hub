## Full description

OpenExam Hub is a study companion for language exams used in immigration, study and work. It covers IELTS, CELPIP, PTE Core, TEF Canada, TCF Canada, DELF, DALF, Goethe, telc and more.

The app includes:

- Exam guides with the skills, format and official resources for each test
- Published study paths you can read on the phone
- Side-by-side comparisons of exams that are often mixed up
- A CLB converter, a CEFR converter, and a CRS language-points calculator, using the same tables as the website
- A study planner that can build a day-by-day plan and save it to your OpenExam Hub account
- A library of official resources, cached on the device after you open it

An account is optional. If you sign in, a plan saved on the website is the same plan in the app. You can delete one plan, or delete the whole account from the app.

Exam guides, study paths, comparisons and the calculators do not show ads. A banner can appear only in the signed-in study planner, and only after you accept or reject the consent question. Rejecting keeps the banner non-personalised and turns analytics off.

OpenExam Hub does not replace the official test or an immigration decision. Scores and points in the calculators are conversions of published tables, not a prediction of your result.
